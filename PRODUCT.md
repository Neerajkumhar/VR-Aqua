# VR AQUA — Product Context

## Product
VR AQUA customizes packaged drinking water bottles with business and event branding. The site is a frontend-only showcase and inquiry funnel for prospective clients.

## Positioning
Custom-labelled packaged drinking water for businesses and events. The bottle is the product; the label carries the brand.

## Audience
Restaurants, hotels, cafés, wedding planners, event organizers, corporate businesses, resorts, banquets, food businesses, and retail brands.

## Primary action
Submit a customization inquiry.

## Content truth
- The only supplied brand assets are 12 bottle designs, each in 500 ML and 1 Litre.
- No prices, testimonials, statistics, client quotes, or contact details were supplied.
- Categories are inferred from the supplied names and must stay broad enough not to assert unverified facts.

## Design direction
Graphite Still Life: a matte graphite field in three close tones, real bottle artwork at full scale on flat product stages, Archivo display against IBM Plex Sans body, one water blue used only for light and state, square corners, hairline rules, and restrained motion. No slogans, lifestyle photography, or decorative apparatus.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: Every paired file is the same design in two sizes.
- ASSUMPTION: The bottle artwork is final and should not be recolored or cropped.
- ASSUMPTION: A static frontend with client-side inquiry composition is acceptable until contact endpoints are supplied.
- ASSUMPTION: The site language is English and the target market is India, based on the supplied brand names.

## Out of scope
Backend, database, authentication, ecommerce checkout, admin dashboard, payment, analytics, fabricated proof, and third-party contact APIs.
